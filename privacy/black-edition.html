Privacy policy

Black Edition · effective 2026-09-27 · version 2.1.0

Short version: Black Edition has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We do not collect, receive or sell any personal data. Everything the app computes, it computes on your device.

1. Who we are

Black Edition is published by Dennis Grobler, South Africa. Questions about this policy: wabapp.support@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Your settings (capital, horizon, risk, fee tier, theme), saved setups, and a short-lived cache of market data are kept in the app's local storage on your device only. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or using Delete my data in the app removes them.

3. What the app downloads

To compute settings the app downloads public market data directly from these providers. Like any internet request, each provider receives your device's IP address and the pair requested; no account, identifier or personal data is sent by the app:

  • Binance — hourly and daily candles, perpetual funding history and the live price stream (privacy)

These providers act independently under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store these requests.

4. Open on your PC (Wi-Fi sharing)

Off unless you switch it on. While on, your phone serves a copy of this app to devices on the same local network at an address with a one-time key, and stops when you switch it off, close the app, or after 30 minutes. Nothing is sent to the internet by this feature, and no data about you is shared — only the app itself.

Permissions

Internet access only, to download market data. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or phone permissions are requested.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process children's data.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; your local data is under your control on your device. You retain your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA, South Africa), the GDPR and similar laws, and may contact us or your data-protection authority at any time.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if this policy changes. Material changes will be shown in the app.
